# Hydraulic Soofi 💧⚙️

**B2B Industrial Hydraulic Solutions**

Hydraulic Soofi (هیدرولیک صوفی) is a professional B2B website for showcasing hydraulic products, engineering services, industrial projects, and technical solutions.

> This is **not an e-commerce platform**. Products are mainly custom-made or quotation-based and do not have public prices, shopping carts, checkout, or online payments.

## ✨ Features

* 🏭 Product catalog & categories
* 🔧 Technical product specifications
* 🛠️ Hydraulic engineering & technical services
* 🏗️ Industrial sectors & applications
* 📋 Projects & portfolio
* 📝 Product inquiries & consultation requests
* 📎 Technical document attachments
* 📚 Educational articles & videos
* ❓ FAQ management
* 🔍 Search & product filtering
* 🔎 SEO metadata
* 👤 Role-based admin management
* 🖼️ Centralized media management

## 🗃️ Architecture

The database is organized into:

```text
Product Catalog
Services & Industries
Projects
Content / CMS
Customer Leads
Administration
Media & SEO
```

Key relationships include:

* Product ↔ Service
* Product ↔ Industry
* Product ↔ Project
* Service ↔ Industry
* Service ↔ Project
* Article ↔ Tags
* Consultation ↔ Project Requirements
